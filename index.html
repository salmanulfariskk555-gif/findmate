ABOUT MIHAS

Meaningful connections.
Built with intention.

We are creating a modern matchmaking experience for adults who value sincerity, compatibility, respect and long-term commitment.

Our mission is simple.

To make it easier to discover people whose values, lifestyle and future intentions align. MIHAS is designed as more than a profile directory: it is a guided experience that places clarity, privacy and genuine compatibility at the centre.

A trusted digital space where adults can explore meaningful compatibility and take the next step towards a lasting relationship.

HELP CENTRE

Questions, answered.

Helpful information about the MIHAS concept and current preview.

Who can use MIHAS?

The intended service is for adults aged 18 and above who are interested in serious relationships and long-term commitment.

Is MIHAS live?

This is an advanced frontend preview. Registration, live discovery, matching, messaging and payments are not active.

Are sample profiles real?

No. All sample profiles and compatibility scores are fictional illustrations.

Will there be a member dashboard?

Yes. A dashboard concept is included here. Secure accounts and real member data require a backend.

Can I pay for membership?

No. Membership plans are concepts only. Payment processing is not connected.

How do I contact the team?

Use the contact form concept below. It does not transmit or store messages.
