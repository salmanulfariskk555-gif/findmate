GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might wonder.

We're building carefully, and we'd rather be clear about what's ready and what's still on the way.

Who is FindMate for?

Adults aged 18 and above who are interested in serious relationships and marriage.

Can I create an account now?

Not yet. This is the public design preview. Registration will open only after verification, privacy and moderation features are ready.

Will my profile be public?

Profiles are designed to stay hidden by default until the member chooses visibility and the profile passes the planned review process.

Is the mobile app ready?

The Android app is in development. This website preview is the first visual step.
